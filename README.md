Da Big Idea:
  A world which resembles a house interior, but without any boundaries. 
  You can walk farther and farther without an end.
  However if you walk too far from the known, things begin to change.
  At first furniture begin to shift and eventually the path begins to reroute and the known becomes the unknown.
  But you are not alone in this wicked maze.
  You will be watched.
  You will be stalked.
